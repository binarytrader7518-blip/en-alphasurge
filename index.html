Alpha Surge Ingredients

Amino acid

Tongkat Ali Root

Eurycoma longifolia

A Southeast Asian root used traditionally for vitality. Several small human trials have studied standardized extracts in men, with mixed but encouraging findings.

Maca Root Extract

Ashwagandha Extract

An Ayurvedic herb studied mainly for stress. NIH reviews note small trials suggesting it may help with perceived stress and sleep.

Horny Goat Weed Extract

A traditional herb that contains icariin. Most research on icariin comes from laboratory and animal studies rather than human trials.
