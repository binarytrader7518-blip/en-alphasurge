L-Arginine and Nitric Oxide

L-Arginine is an amino acid found in protein foods. The body uses it to make nitric oxide, a signalling molecule that helps the smooth muscle in blood vessel walls relax. MedlinePlus explains this mechanism and reviews the evidence by use.

What the Evidence Says

It’s worth being honest here. The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements notes that arginine supplementation has little scientific support for improving athletic performance. MedlinePlus lists some uses with modest evidence and others with little or none. In Alpha Surge, L-Arginine is one part of a broader formula positioned to support healthy circulation — not a treatment.*

Check with your doctor first if you take blood pressure medicine, nitrates or erectile dysfunction medication, as L-Arginine may interact with them.

Circulation-Friendly Habits

  • Regular cardio — even brisk walking — most days of the week.
  • Not smoking.
  • Eating nitrate-rich vegetables such as leafy greens and beets.
  • Keeping blood pressure and cholesterol checked.
