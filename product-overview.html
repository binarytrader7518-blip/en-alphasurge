The Five Alpha Surge Ingredients

As listed by the seller. Exact amounts appear on the Supplement Facts panel of the bottle.

L-Arginine HCl

Amino acid

An amino acid the body uses to make nitric oxide, a molecule involved in relaxing blood vessels. Included to support healthy circulation as part of the formula.

Tongkat Ali Root

Eurycoma longifolia

A Southeast Asian root used traditionally for vitality. Several small human trials have studied standardized extracts in men, with mixed but encouraging findings.

Maca Root Extract

Lepidium meyenii

An Andean root vegetable. A 12-week trial in healthy men reported improved self-rated sexual desire, without changes in testosterone levels.

Ashwagandha Extract

Withania somnifera

An Ayurvedic herb studied mainly for stress. NIH reviews note small trials suggesting it may help with perceived stress and sleep.

Horny Goat Weed Extract

Epimedium

A traditional herb that contains icariin. Most research on icariin comes from laboratory and animal studies rather than human trials.
