Tongkat Ali: What the Research Says About This Malaysian Root

By the Alpha Surge Editorial Team · Updated 25 November 2026

Tongkat ali (Eurycoma longifolia) is a shrub-like tree native to Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand. Its root has been used for generations as a tonic for energy and male vitality, and today it’s one of the most popular botanicals in men’s supplements — including Alpha Surge.

What Has Been Studied?

The most cited human study is a 12-week randomized, placebo-controlled trial of a standardized water extract called Physta, published in 2012. It enrolled 109 men aged 30 to 55. Men taking the extract reported improvements on quality-of-life and sexual well-being questionnaires compared with placebo. A separate small crossover study in 13 recreational athletes found six weeks of 400 mg/day did not change liver or kidney function markers.

What the Research Can’t Tell You

  • The trials used a specific, standardized extract at defined doses. Other products may use different extracts or amounts.
  • Most studies are small and short.
  • Results from one ingredient don’t prove that a multi-ingredient formula works.
Talk to your doctor before using tongkat ali if you have a hormone-sensitive condition, take prescription medication or have liver or kidney concerns.

The Bottom Line

Tongkat ali has more human research behind it than many vitality herbs, but the evidence is still early. Treat it as one part of a healthy routine — sleep, strength training and good food do the heavy lifting.
